| Mode         | Voice purpose            | Approx. voice |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------- |
| **Discover** | Introduce the experience | 25–45 sec     |
| **Remember** | Bring it back to memory  | 8–15 sec      |
| **Trust**    | One gentle doorway       | 2–6 sec       |
| **Embody**   | No voice                 | —             |


And importantly, the voice doesn't need to explain the entire practice every day. The music and walking do most of the work.

The actual scripts
1. Opening Your Heart
    
# Discover

Take a breath... and begin to walk.

Feel your feet meeting the ground.
Feel your breath moving through you.

And gently bring your attention toward your heart.

You don't have to create a feeling.
You don't have to make anything happen.

Simply notice what is already here.

Let yourself be here... exactly as you are.

# Remember

Begin gently.

Feel your breath. Feel your steps.

And open your heart to whatever is already here.

# Trust

Open your heart.

# Embody

No voice.

2. Feeling Your Power

# Discover

Feel your body as you walk.

Feel your feet.
Feel your breath.
Feel your movement.

There is strength in simply being here...
alive, awake, and moving forward.

You don't have to force yourself to feel powerful.

Just notice what is already here.

Feel it.

# Remember

Feel your body moving.

Let your movement remind you of the strength that's already here.

# Trust

Feel your power.

# Embody

No voice.

3. Letting Go & Total Presence

# Discover

For these next few moments...
you don't need to solve anything.

You don't need to figure anything out.

Let your thoughts come and go.

Come back to your next step.
Feel the air.
Hear the world around you.
Feel your breath.

And when your mind wanders...
that's okay.

Gently come back.

Come back to this step.
Come back to now.

# Remember

Nothing needs to be solved right now.

Feel your next step.
Feel your breath.

Come back to now.

# Trust

Come back to now.

# Embody

No voice.

4. Connecting with Higher Power

This one should remain beautifully open.

# Discover

Now... make a little room for something greater than yourself.

Maybe you call it God.
Maybe you call it the universe.
Maybe it's nature... love... or simply the mystery of being alive.

And if you don't believe in anything beyond yourself...
that's okay too.

You don't need to define it.
You don't need to understand it.

Just make room.

Open yourself to whatever feels meaningful to you...
and let yourself walk with it.

# Remember

Make a little room for what is meaningful to you.

You don't need to name it.

Just connect in whatever way feels true to you.

# Trust

Make room.

# Embody

No voice.

5. Celebrate & Raise Your Vibration

# Discover

And now... notice that you showed up.

You gave yourself this time.
You walked.
You breathed.
You came back to yourself.

Let yourself appreciate that.

Maybe you feel peace.
Maybe joy.
Maybe gratitude.
Maybe something you don't have a name for.

You don't need to hold onto it.

Just let yourself feel it.

Celebrate being here.

And carry whatever you found with you...
into the rest of your day.

# Remember

Notice that you showed up for yourself today.

Celebrate being here.

Carry whatever is here with you into the rest of your day.

# Trust

Celebrate being here.

# Embody

No voice.
